The iconic Porsche crest was created in late 1952 by Franz Xaver Reimspieß. It combines the prancing horse from Stuttgart's city seal with the red-and-black colors and stylized antlers of the former people's state of Württemberg-Hohenzollern, honoring the brand's home and heritage.
