CV · Editor's Pick
Dune: Part Two
DIR. DENIS VILLENEUVE -- SCI-FI / EPIC
Sci-Fi Epic

A sequel that out-scales its own ambition

Villeneuve trades the first film's restraint for spectacle, and the gamble pays off. The Fremen sequences on the open sand feel genuinely vast, shot with a patience that modern blockbusters rarely allow themselves.

Where it truly lands is Chani's arc -- the film's emotional spine -- giving the back half a weight the trailers never hinted at. It's dense, occasionally punishing, and one of the few franchise sequels that earns its runtime.

★★★★★
4.5 / 5 · CineView Staff Review
